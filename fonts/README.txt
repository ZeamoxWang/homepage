Yi Yi Handwriting

Quote-only WOFF2 subset of Yozai Regular v0.868 by LXGW / Y.Oz.
File: yi-yi-yozai.woff2
Upstream: https://github.com/lxgw/yozai-font
Source: https://github.com/lxgw/yozai-font/releases/download/v0.868/Yozai-Regular.ttf
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1. See OFL-Yozai.txt for the complete license.

Created with fontTools. Glyph outlines are unchanged. Family and PostScript
names identify this subset as Yi Yi Handwriting. Copyright and license
metadata are retained in the font.

Coverage: the Chinese Yi Yi quotation in index.html, its Chinese title, and
space. If that text changes, regenerate the subset from the upstream TTF.
Characters outside the subset fall back to the existing Chinese font stack.

The previous Xiaolai subset (yi-yi-handwriting.woff2) is retained with its
OFL-Xiaolai.txt license for older cached pages. It is no longer used by index.html.
